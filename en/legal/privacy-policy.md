"Affirmation Alarm" (hereinafter the "App") respects your privacy and handles information in accordance with the policy below.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information in any form accessible to the developer.

The following information created within the App is stored on your device:

- Alarm times
- Repeat days of the week
- Affirmation text used to dismiss alarms
- Selected alarm sound
- Some cached alarm settings and premium status
- Display language setting

This information is not transmitted to the developer's servers or to any third-party service.

## 2. Use of Notification Permission

The App uses the notification permission to display alarm notifications and play notification sounds at the times you set. You can change the notification permission at any time in your device settings.

## 3. Use of AlarmKit

On supported iOS environments, the App may use AlarmKit for the purpose of ringing alarms you created in the App at the specified times. Use of AlarmKit is limited to providing the alarm feature.

## 4. Purchase Information

The App uses Apple's StoreKit to provide premium features. Purchases, billing, refunds, and subscription management are handled by Apple. The developer does not obtain your payment card number, Apple ID, billing information, or similar data.

The App may store a cache of purchase status on your device in order to determine whether premium features are active.

## 5. Third-Party SDKs, Advertising, and Analytics

The App does not use any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or external tracking SDKs.

## 6. External Transmission of Data

The App does not transmit alarm information, affirmation text, usage history, device identifiers, or similar data to the developer or any third party.

## 7. Deletion of Data

Alarm information created within the App can be deleted within the App. In addition, when you delete the App from your device, the App's data stored on the device is also deleted.

For cancellation or refunds of subscriptions, please use Apple ID subscription management or Apple Support.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Disclaimer

Depending on the device state, notification settings, Focus mode, volume, OS limitations, battery state, and other factors, the App may not display notifications or play sounds as expected. Do not rely solely on the App for important schedules or matters involving life, body, or property.

## 10. Revisions

This policy may be revised as necessary. If there are significant changes, we will provide notice within the App or on the public pages.
