For inquiries regarding Affirmation Alarm, please contact us at the following:

Email: support@ryukoeng.com

When you contact us, please include the following information as much as possible:

- Device used
- iOS version
- App version
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce the issue

## Frequently Checked Items

If your alarm does not ring, please check the following:

- That notifications for this App are allowed in your iOS notification settings
- That the device volume is turned up sufficiently
- That it is not affected by Focus mode, Do Not Disturb, or the silent setting
- That the target alarm is enabled within the App

You can cancel or manage your subscription from the official Apple page below:

https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
